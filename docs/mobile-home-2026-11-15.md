# Mobile product home — 15 November 2026

The live Roby's home was reviewed for its distinct introduction, moment-based
paths and direct order action. Its claim of popularity and unsolicited promotional
overlay are not carried into Poçitel. Poçitel's prior mobile CSS hid the hero
photo/actions and opened with category controls; the desktop hero photo did not
reach the mobile first screen.

Poçitel's proposed positioning is **focaccia, brunch and coffee**. This is an
editorial product choice based on the existing menu, existing çılbır identity and
the user's explicit preference for focaccia. It is not a finding about sales,
food cost, popularity or profit. Keep the positioning open to revision after
owner feedback and paid-sale evidence.

The new home has three explicit manual choices: focaccia, brunch (çılbır) and
coffee (cappuccino). Focaccia opens first. Each shows its actual catalog price,
catalog composition, large complete image and a direct details/choose action.
Existing illustration labels are retained; no new synthetic food photo was
created or misrepresented as the café's own photograph. There is no timed
rotation, forced countdown or automatic addition. A nearby focaccia + drink
action opens the existing editable pairing builder.

The startup overlay is no longer requested by the page; the product is visible
directly. Menu navigation, cart, share links, noindex, TR/EN/RU, paid-only metrics
and the owner-workspace security boundary remain in place. Registration never
blocks browsing or ordering. See `customer-account-setup.md` for the explicit
guest-vs-account boundary.

Acceptance covers 320/390/768px in three languages, loaded imagery, visible first
screen action, keyboard choices, product details, pairing builder, storage failure,
local preference persistence/reset and no outbound identity requests in guest mode.
Browser engines/emulation do not constitute physical-device or conversion evidence.
