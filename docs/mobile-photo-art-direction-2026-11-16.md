and portrait files. On a 390px phone the portrait çılbır became a narrow central
strip, while the focaccia and coffee images had side gutters. Switching products
changed their apparent scale despite representing equal choices.

The product cover now uses a consistent 3:2 frame with the image filling the
frame. Focaccia and cappuccino keep their existing landscape catalog artwork.
Çılbır uses the existing 640/960px landscape crops of the same dish instead of the
portrait file. No dish, ingredient, new image, sales claim or price was invented.
The existing illustration disclosure moves into the caption below the image.

Mobile home width is capped at 540px to avoid oversized photos between phone
and desktop layouts. The selected category uses a quiet underline; the primary
action remains visually strongest. Labels identify the selected product, and
the body reserves room for two-line names/descriptions without truncation.
The full-ingredient product viewer and complete-image cart behavior remain
separate from the editorial home crop.

Acceptance: loaded landscape covers fill both axes, all three cover frames keep
the same size while switching, readable price/details, TR/EN/RU at
320/360/390/430/768px, primary action visible on 844px-high phones, and reachable
on a shorter 640px viewport. Manual switching and keyboard interaction remain;
reduced motion disables the brief opacity transition. No auto-rotation is added.

Noindex is retained. Browser/emulation checks do not prove a physical-device
review or measured conversion gain.
