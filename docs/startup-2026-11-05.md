# Poçitel welcome screen

The welcome screen pairs the existing branded takeaway cappuccino photo with the featured çılbır photo and the café logo. It follows the selected appearance and Turkish, English or Russian language. The cup gently floats and three small dots animate; reduced-motion settings disable animation and the brief minimum presentation time.

The standalone early `startup.js` shows a decorative overlay while the menu initializes. With artwork ready, the ordinary brief presentation is 650 ms from startup plus a 220 ms fade. An independent 1,600 ms deadline starts the same fade even if DOM readiness, the menu module or image decoding stalls. A pointer press or key press removes it immediately. It neither captures pointer events nor locks scrolling/focus. Product share links keep opening their requested item directly; a restored back/forward page does not replay the welcome. With the script unavailable or JavaScript disabled, the `hidden` markup remains hidden.

No new image requests to external services or generated assets are required: the three existing local images total approximately 78 KiB. No persistent setting or tracking record is written. All 162 static sharing pages were regenerated from the updated shell, including the new script and CSS revision.

Validation: 9 existing nutrition and sharing tests passed; startup JavaScript syntax and whitespace checks passed. A temporary, source-based smoke check confirmed the maximum deadline without DOM readiness, immediate pointer skip, direct-item bypass, Russian selection and blocked-storage fallback. Browser QA confirmed automatic removal, a working coffee filter after removal, the frozen visual component at 390×844 and 320×568, and the dark component at 568×320. The 390 px screenshot was visually inspected, but its file could not be synchronized for attachment. The frozen visual fixture does not imply that the ordinary welcome stays open indefinitely. Temporary visual QA routes were removed before publication. Native device testing was not performed.
