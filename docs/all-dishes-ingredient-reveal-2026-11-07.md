# Ingredient reveal for the food menu

The interactive ingredient reveal covers the 11 main food items in the breakfast, sweet-and-bites, and focaccia categories, as well as the food in all four fixed menu pairings. It also works on the existing takeaway food variants. Drinks and optional extras remain in their existing nutrition views.

Ingredient labels are based on the café's printed menu and are available in Turkish, English, and Russian. Menu alternatives are labelled as alternatives rather than as ingredients present together. The three focaccias have an illustrated outside/inside comparison; the other dishes reveal a labelled version of their existing food image. The view identifies AI illustrations where they are used, and explicitly says the labels are based on the menu.

Automated tests check that every main food item and pairing has the correct coverage, that localized labels exist, and that each fixed pairing references the food used by the nutrition data.
