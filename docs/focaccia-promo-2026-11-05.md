# Focaccia + iced Americano to-go offer

The menu now offers a compact photo popup for the existing Lunch Favourite pairing: smoked-turkey focaccia plus iced Americano, 395 ₺ versus 415 ₺ separately in the supplied menu. Its price and saving are read from the catalog. The photos illustrate the menu items; takeaway packaging and availability remain subject to confirmation by the café. The popup does not submit an order or take payment: its action adds a selection and opens the existing order review / waiter flow.

A menu banner opens the popup explicitly. Automatic display is limited to once per browser-tab session, after the menu becomes visible (8 seconds), or after choosing takeaway (600 ms). It does not interrupt another dialog, a hidden tab, text entry, a visitor outside the menu, an unrelated existing cart, an already selected lunch pairing, or a direct product link. Closing is available through the close control, the secondary action and native Escape. Manual reopening remains available while the pairing is not selected.

The offer reuses one matching selected focaccia and/or iced Americano rather than adding duplicates, retaining other portions and any chosen syrup. It shows the replacement and exact price delta before confirmation. The resulting catalog variant is `to-go-lunch-favourite`; the existing configurator, cart persistence, nutritional panel, sharing links and Turkish waiter handoff preserve its serving preference. A transaction-bound undo restores the prior selection while the cart has not otherwise changed. A subsequent cart edit invalidates that undo to avoid overwriting newer choices.

The catalog now has 55 entries: 46 menu entries and 9 takeaway variants. The additional variant retains the pairing's existing menu photo and price; its picture is not asserted to be actual takeaway packaging. All 165 localized share pages and the generated calorie summary were rebuilt.

JavaScript syntax and whitespace checks passed.
